These are existing third-party assets, not AI-generated models. Previews and measurements are produced locally by Noobi. Use only when their low-poly style matches the selected plan. Models contain no gameplay collision or guaranteed semantic facing. Validate scale, collision and visual fit in the target scene. No donation, account or API request is needed at runtime.
